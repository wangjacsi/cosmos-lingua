# Lingua Live

Practise real conversations and return to the expressions you want to remember.
Lingua keeps your learning goals and progress for each language, so you can pick
up where you left off without explaining your background again.

## What it does

- Practise everyday conversations or role-play situations such as travel and work.
- See corrections and useful expressions while your conversation continues.
- Review expressions with your own sentence, compare an example, and choose what needs more practice.
- See your saved expressions and recorded review checks in your learning overview.
- Keep separate goals, interests and progress for each language you study.

Useful when you want more confidence speaking, examples that fit your interests,
and a clear next step when you come back to practise.

## Setup

Install Lingua Live from the Cosmos Market, then open its learning settings.
Choose a target language, your level and a goal. Choose the explanation language
separately from the language you are learning. English is the default documentation
language; the interface follows your Cosmos language selection.

Start a practice session and use Cosmos chat or voice controls to talk. Open the
Lingua view alongside the conversation to see feedback and revisit expressions.
In the development version, choose Learning overview or Focus on practice, and
open the view inside Cosmos, in an independent window, or in the external browser.

## How it works

Lingua uses your learning preferences and previous practice to guide the next
conversation. Repeated difficulties become material to revisit. Review charts
reflect recorded self-checks; they are not a fluency score or certification.

## What it needs

Memory access lets Lingua retain your goals, progress and recurring difficulties
and recall them for future practice. Conversation and voice use your configured
Cosmos services. No separate Lingua API key is required.

## Data and privacy

Goals, progress summaries and recurring difficulties can be stored in Cosmos memory.
Detailed expressions and review schedules stay in your user-specific learning state,
separated by target language. Practice analysis uses the configured model service.
Draft review sentences remain in the open view; only the self-check is saved.
Removing Lingua retains learning records and preferences for reinstallation.

## What it does NOT do

Lingua does not certify your level, guarantee exam results or automatically grade
review drafts. Video discovery, subtitles and scene-based video learning are not
currently verified features. Opening the view does not turn on a microphone.

## Limits

Version 0.6.0 is the published baseline. The latest display and lifecycle improvements
are development changes and have not been released. Real voice/video learning and
published update validation remain pending for these changes.
